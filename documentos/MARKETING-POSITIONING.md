### 4.2 Positioning Statements

**vs GitHub Copilot:**
```
"GitHub Copilot is autocomplete on steroids. SquadX Live is a 
full development squad working together with your team, with 
live visibility, collaboration, and transparency."

When they say: "We already have Copilot"
We say: "Great! SquadX complements Copilot. While Copilot helps 
individual devs write code faster, SquadX manages entire features 
end-to-end with a squad of specialized agents, all supervised 
by your team in real-time."
```

**vs Cursor:**
```
"Cursor is a great AI-powered IDE for individuals. SquadX Live 
is designed for teams, with multi-agent orchestration, live 
multi-viewer sessions, and built-in collaboration tools."

When they say: "We use Cursor"
We say: "Cursor is excellent for single-developer workflows. 
SquadX shines when you need multiple agents (frontend, backend, 
DevOps) working in parallel, with your whole team watching and 
collaborating live."
```

**vs Devin:**
```
"Devin is a black box in the cloud. SquadX Live gives you full 
transparency through live screen sharing, keeps your code local, 
and lets your team collaborate on agent supervision."

When they say: "What about Devin?"
We say: "Devin is opaque - you don't see what it's doing until 
it's done, and your code goes to their cloud. SquadX Live lets 
you WATCH agents work in real-time, intervene when needed, and 
your code never leaves your infrastructure."
```

**vs Building In-House:**
```
"Building your own AI agent platform would take 12-24 months 
and cost $500K-$2M in engineering time. SquadX Live is ready now, 
battle-tested, and costs a fraction of that."

When they say: "We can build this ourselves"
We say: "You absolutely could. But it took us 8 months and 3 
engineers to build the multi-agent orchestration, live view, 
and collaboration features. Is that your core business? Or would 
you rather use that engineering time building your actual product?"
```
