## 11. Risk Mitigation

### Business Risks & Mitigation

```
Risk 1: Low adoption of Live View
├─ Mitigation: Make it default ON, easy to use
├─ Alternative: Strong recording/replay value
└─ Metric: Track usage weekly, pivot if <20% adoption

Risk 2: High infrastructure costs
```
